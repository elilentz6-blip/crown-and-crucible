## 1. The Realm of Aethelgard

Across the wind-swept archipelago of Aethelgard, the Crown Reckoning marks the year 487. A hundred years of intermittent raids, border skirmishes, and uneasy truces are collapsing into open civil war. 

To the east, King Aldous III holds the royal seat of Maroven, enforcing royal tallage and the King’s Peace along the arterial rivers. To the west, the rebellious Iron Council of Dunhollow gathers disgruntled mining barons, disenfranchised sappers, and veteran mercenary freeblades, determined to shatter royal taxation by force of iron. Between them lies the Greenmere Vale—a border march of timber bridges, limestone barrows, watermills, and modest farming manors where common folk pray for peace and prepare for famine.

In **Crown & Crucible**, you do not arrive as a foretold champion, a sorcerer of ancient lineage, or a lord in shining armor. You play as **Rowan**—an experienced, common-born man-at-arms with calloused hands, a dented kettle-helm, an iron arming sword, and an empty coin purse. 

There are no dragons to slay. There are no magical portals or forgotten curses. In Aethelgard, danger wears mail and gambeson, and the most devastating weapon is not a lightning bolt, but a winter grain embargo.

### I. Spatial Integrity & 17 Enterable Building Interiors
The hamlet of Stonecross is not a painted facade. Every functional structure in the settlement—from Master Odo's smoldering forge and Baker Beatrice’s stone ovens to the Parish Tithe Barn, Weaver’s loft, and King's Highroad Toll Gatehouse—is a physically enterable space with its own dedicated layout, furnishings, and interactive props. Doorways feature engineered geometric threshold clearances that completely eliminate transition bouncing and spawn loops.

### II. The Rhythm of Life: 4-Phase Daily Routines
The people of Stonecross do not stand frozen waiting for your arrival. Over the course of **Dawn**, **Midday**, **Dusk**, and **Night**, all primary inhabitants follow living schedules:
- **Master Odo** strikes glowing billets at dawn, tends the grindstone at midday, and shares a flagon of bitter ale at the tavern by dusk.
- **Farmer Giles** weeds the cabbage patch under the morning sun, hauls grain sacks to the tithe barn in the afternoon, and latches his farmhouse shutters against nightfall.
- **Brother Dunstan** chants matins in the chapel, tends medicinal herb beds in the churchyard, and treats wounded travellers by candlelight.

### III. Deep Reactive Dialogue & Social Memory
With 37 stateful branching conversation trees (28 characters and 9 historical site inspections), dialogue is a living negotiation. Townsfolk notice your injuries, track unpaid tavern tabs on Barnaby's chalk slate, react to royal proclamations, and judge you by your deeds. You are never addressed as an unearned "lord" or "knight"; you are a man-at-arms whose reputation must be forged contract by contract.

### IV. Honest Commoner Livelihoods & Tenancy
War is costly, and blood is not the only currency. Rowan can earn honest coin by hiring out his muscle across 6 grounded regional occupations:
- **Field Farm Labor**: Threshing rye and ditching furrows for Farmer Giles (+8 Gold).
- **Smithy Forge Labor**: Working the bellows and stoking coal for Master Odo (+14 Gold).
- **Town Watch Shifts**: Manning the palisade gates alongside Watchman Wulf (+10 Gold).
- **Quarry Cartage**: Hauling limestone blocks across the barrow roads (+18 Gold).
- **Forest Hunting**: Tracking wild game through the deep timber tracks (+15 Gold).
- **Wild Herbal Foraging**: Gathering marsh herbs and roots for the apothecary (+12 Gold).

By accumulating savings and proving trustworthiness, Rowan can acquire formal agricultural tenancy leases, progressing from an itinerant day-laborer to a recognized factor receiving daily dividend yields.

### V. Systemic Economy & The Rule 13 Royal Assize of Bread
Markets in Aethelgard operate on dynamic local supply-and-demand elasticity. Flood the stalls with iron ingots, and their price plunges; buy out the miller’s flour, and regional prices soar. Furthermore:
- **Cargo Transport Tiers**: Rowan cannot carry an armory on his back. You begin with a humble **3-sack Foot Pack**, upgrade to a **10-sack Pack Mule**, and eventually invest in a heavy **30-sack Carter Wagon**.
- **The Royal Assize of Bread**: As civil war escalates and the War Clock ticks forward, the Crown invokes statutory emergency measures. In Crown settlements (*Stonecross* and *High Cairn*), grain prices are legally capped at **<= 18 Gold** to prevent famine and speculation, while rebel markets in *Dunhollow* suffer runaway inflation past 30 Gold.

### VI. Tactical Consequential Combat & Lasting Scars
Combat combines precision rhythm-timed melee offense across 5 historically authentic weapon classes (Arming Sword, Greatsword, Dagger, Hunting Bow, and Poleaxe/Spear) with real-time dodging and parrying in a defense arena:
- **Physical Exertion**: Every weapon swing burns stamina and inflicts wear on weapon edges.
- **Armor Integrity**: Taking unblocked strikes gouges armor plates and tears chainmail, degrading damage reduction until repaired.
- **No Free Full Heals**: Defeat does not magically reset your health. If Rowan collapses, a passing carter drags him from the mud to Brother Dunstan's clinic. You awaken at **50% HP**, nursing **Bruised Ribs** that penalize stamina recovery, with damaged armor and a medical salvage debt recorded on the chapel ledger.

### VII. Rowan's Diegetic 5-Tab Parchment Journal
Pressing **[J]** opens Rowan's personal handwritten notebook, rendered as authentic folded parchment:
1. **What I've Learned**: Historical chronicles, regional origins, and church records.
2. **Papers I Carry**: Royal warrants, safe-conducts, merchant receipts, and bailiff pardon writs.
3. **What People Say**: Overheard rumors, tavern gossip, and local anxieties.
4. **Work & Contracts**: Feudal military contracts, bounty notices, and merchant deliveries.
5. **Debts & Standing**: Regional reputations (Crown, Dunhollow, River League), chivalric renown, military rank, and active debt ledgers.

## 3. The Seven Isles of Aethelgard

According to the surviving transcripts of **Oren of Halewick** (*Chronicles of Aethelgard*, CR 487), the archipelago comprises seven distinct island provinces:

| Island | Regional Identity | Character & Landscape | Strategic Significance |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Maroven** | The Royal Heartland | Rolling wheat plains, limestone bluffs, royal highroads, and the walled citadel of High Cairn. | Seat of King Aldous III; enforces royal grain tolls and high justice. |
| **Caerwyn** | The Chivalric Valleys | Ancient river gorges, tournament pavilions, and weathered stone boundary watchposts. | Home to proud feudal marcher lords whose loyalties are divided. |
| **Rusk** | The Iron Ridge | Dark pine hills, blast furnaces, limestone quarries, and deep iron pits. | Source of military spring steel, master sappers, and heavy armor. |
| **Ordel** | The Breadbasket | Expansive rye fields, parish tithe barns, and monastic granges. | Supplies the grain that feeds the armies; prime target for wartime blockades. |
| **Terveth** | The River Estuaries | Interlocking tidal channels, timber wharves, and independent bargemasters. | Controls inland water shipping and smuggling routes between islands. |
| **Sere** | The Windward Coast | Rugged slate sea cliffs, herring smokehouses, and hardy fishing hamlets. | Provides maritime pilots and salted provisions; fiercely autonomous. |
| **Vey** | The Outer Marches | Deep birch taiga, overgrown royal redoubts, and ancient burial mounds. | The untamed border where deserters, renegades, and outlaws hide. |

## 4. Technical Craftsmanship

Crown & Crucible is engineered from the ground up in **GameMaker 2024.14** as a native macOS application bundle, combining retro-tactile 2D aesthetic sensibilities with modern systemic simulation architecture:

- **Dual-Grid Autotiling Engine**: Seamless procedural rendering of cobblestone tracks, muddy drover roads, limestone banks, and water shorelines without visual seam stutter.
- **Dynamic Time-of-Day Shader Pipeline**: Authentic solar angle illumination shifting smoothly across morning gold, noon glare, amber dusk, and lantern-lit midnight.
