# Provenance: art_src/characters

All PNGs in this folder are original work, generated procedurally by `gen_characters.py`
(written for Crown & Crucible, October 2026) from the definitions in `defs/`.
No third-party sprites, tiles, photos or AI image models were used, and no existing game's
artwork was traced or copied. Tools: Python 3 + Pillow (HPND licence) at build time only.
